# Three-minute demonstration

Show one synthetic job with twenty copies of the owned `samples/demo-block.stl`: Meta intake, independent A1/A1 mini/X2D quotes, initial approval, and cross-printer recovery after a simulated pre-start outage. Use a fresh rehearsal database and a deadline at least three days ahead.

| Time | Screen action and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Introduce the quoting and outage-planning problem. |
| 0:15–0:40 | Extract a draft with Meta, resolve missing units, review quantity 20 and deadline, select the required sample model file, and create the job. |
| 0:40–1:05 | The sample was attached during job creation. In Cross-printer quote adviser, select A1, A1 mini and X2D. Review each saved preset and support share. Confirm settings, then Slice and compare selected printers. Show three independent estimates, prices and schedules. |
| 1:05–1:25 | Permit sharing the displayed totals and scheduling facts, then Compare verified options with Meta. Show actual reasons and model attribution; no option is auto-selected or committed. |
| 1:25–1:40 | Select the X2D for this scenario, check the exact-price/reservation approval box, and click Approve price & reservation. |
| 1:40–2:10 | Simulate printer outage. In the cross-printer adviser, request fresh independent slices for all three profiles. Permit data sharing again and ask Meta to compare. Show actual delays, deadlines and price differences. |
| 2:10–2:35 | Select an on-time alternative with reviewed supports. Pause on the original quote/reservation, which remain unchanged. Check the exact-price/reservation approval box, then Approve price & reservation. Show the replacement and new quote. |
| 2:35–2:50 | Show history, including comparison explained and comparison approved. State that stale results require a new comparison. |
| 2:50–3:00 | Close on operator control over price and capacity. |

Use clearly labeled synthetic pricing for the public recording. The rehearsal launcher initially copies the local rate card: replace it through Shop rates **in the rehearsal only**, before creating quotes or requesting Meta comparison. Do not show private shop rates or send private-rate-derived totals to Meta without explicit permission. Twenty copies help avoid identical minimum-order prices hiding differences; verify the actual results during rehearsal rather than promising a particular price change.

The cross-printer feature requires separately imported supported profiles; they are not bundled. Review quality-related preset differences as well as time and price. Enter support share 0 only after verifying the sample generates no support for each selected profile. Unknown support review may block approval. Parts are scheduled sequentially; this is not batch nesting.

Meta selects recommendations and allowed reasons from verified slice, quote and schedule facts; the server validates and renders the explanation. The scheduler and price arithmetic are application logic. Sharing totals with Meta, selecting a proposal, and approving the exact price/reservation are three distinct decisions. Historical quotes remain recorded. Cross-printer recovery can apply a newly approved price; same-profile recovery is a different path that preserves its quote.

Do not use Propose recovery / Explain recovery options with Meta / Approve recovery for this recording: those controls demonstrate the earlier same-profile workflow. Use the Cross-printer quote adviser and its Approve price & reservation button for both commitments.

All timing ranges are edit targets. Rehearse before recording and trim only waits, with a visible “wait shortened” caption. Keep actions and actual results from the same run. Do not claim Meta recommended a particular printer or reason unless it did. Choose an alternative with a visibly different price if available; otherwise state that the verified totals are equal. Regenerate stale comparisons and never force an infeasible deadline. No physical printer is controlled and no customer message is sent.

Private recordings, imported profiles, local rates and raw verification reports stay outside the public repository. Add the final video URL and update submission timestamps after editing.
